# Evalnor Talent product vision

Talent combines ATS/recruiting with optional HR lifecycle modules. It uses Evalnor Core for generic Workforce, Tasks, Documents, Party, permissions, audit and notifications.

## Recruiting workspace
Open positions, recruitment requests, assigned recruiters, customizable Kanban pipelines, candidate profiles, CV bank, reusable forms/email templates, bulk actions, career-site publishing connectors and analytics.

## Candidate -> employee
Hiring is an explicit reviewed transition. The operator chooses which allowed candidate data is transferred into the workforce profile; recruiting history is not blindly copied into the employee record.

## HR lifecycle
Optional employee records, onboarding/offboarding protocols, performance/development, employee portal, documentation and internal communications. Each module can be disabled without deleting data or breaking recruiting.

## UX
Recruiters land on pipeline/work requiring attention. HR lands on arrivals/departures, approvals and overdue actions. Employees land on their own schedule/tasks/documents/requests. Avoid one giant admin dashboard for every role.

## Fairness/privacy
No opaque ranking of people. AI may summarize or assist with drafting/search, but consequential hiring/employment decisions remain human-reviewed and sensitive HR data is permission-separated.
